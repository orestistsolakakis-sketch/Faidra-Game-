# Level 01 — Cinder Hollow

> **Opening level & tutorial region.** Arlen's home. Teaches her first three
> ability tiers, establishes tone and world, and ends by forcing the Arlen ↔
> Lysandra partnership that drives the game. Canon: see
> [`WORLD_BIBLE.md`](../WORLD_BIBLE.md) §12 (Cinder Hollow's symptoms) and
> [`CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`](../CHARACTER_BIBLE.md) (Arlen L1–L3, Romance Phase 1→2).

---

## 1. Fantasy & first impression

A lower-city district built **down** into old mining tunnels and the bones of
abandoned industrial machinery — lamplit, steam-veined, lived-in. Homes are
welded into gantries; a century of repairs is visible in every wall. It should
feel **warm and human at the surface, uneasy the deeper you go.** The player's
first minutes should say: *these are real people, this is a real place, and
something here is quietly going wrong.*

This is not a threatening horror opening. The dread is **environmental and slow**
(your light-horror pillar): lights flicker in patterns that feel almost
intentional, a sealed tunnel *breathes* warm air, a machine no one built is
humming somewhere below.

## 2. Role in the game

- **Act 1 opening.** First playable region. Player is **Arlen only** — Lysandra
  is not yet met (she arrives at the climax of this level).
- **Teaches:** movement, interaction, **Repair (L1)**, **Sense Damage (L2)**, and
  the story-critical **first Animal heal (L3)**.
- **Ends on** the inciting incident that forces Arlen out of Cinder Hollow and
  into partnership with Lysandra.

## 3. Cast present

- **Arlen Kufstein** — player character.
- **Arlen's best friend** *(name TBD)* — introduced here; comic warmth, teases,
  gives the tutorial its heart and later the "you look at her differently" runner.
- **A sick neighbor / elder** — the human face of Cinder Hollow's illness
  symptom; motivates the descent.
- **Lysandra Kufstein** — appears only at the **climax** (pursued, out of place,
  crashing into Arlen's world). No control of her yet.

## 4. Main objective

> **"Something below Cinder Hollow is making people sick. Find out what — and
> shut it off."**

The surface story is a practical mechanic's problem (a failing old machine
poisoning the district). The real answer — a fragment of the Heart Engine's
network waking up as the Engine fails — is only hinted at here and paid off later.

## 5. Critical path (beats + what each teaches)

| Beat | Location | Teaches / does |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Home & hands** | Arlen's workshop | Movement, camera, **Interact**. Fix a jammed workshop door → first **Repair (L1)**. Best friend banter establishes voice. |
| **B. The district** | Cinder Hollow surface | Free exploration hub. NPCs describe the sickness and the "noise from below." Optional side beats seeded (see §8). Objective: reach the lower tunnels. |
| **C. Sealed descent** | Tunnel mouth | A dead freight elevator blocks the way. **Repair puzzle** to restore it (see §7, P1). Introduces resource/tool loop. |
| **D. First Sense** | Upper tunnels | Damage is hidden behind walls. Arlen touches a conduit and **Sense (L2)** triggers for the first time — a diegetic "vision" reveals the broken layout. Now a traversal + discovery tool. |
| **E. The growth** | Deep tunnels | The "strange magical growth" (WB §12): Lifeline plant-matter fused to waking machinery. Environmental storytelling peak. Light-horror mood high. |
| **F. The animal** | A collapsed gallery | An animal (a mine-creature) is trapped and badly hurt. Arlen tries **ordinary** first-aid — it fails. On touch, the wound closes on its own. **First Animal heal (L3).** She panics, pulls back, healing stops. *She has no idea what she did.* **The emotional core of the level.** |
| **G. The source** | The old machine core | The thing making people sick: a fragment of Engine-network machinery, over-full of trapped magic (WB §2 "trapped magic becomes unstable"). **Mini-boss** (see §9). |
| **H. Collision** | Surface, aftermath | Lysandra crashes into Cinder Hollow — pursued, terrified, unmistakably royal. The two meet on the **worst** terms (Phase 1 Distrust). Level ends: they must leave together. |

## 6. Unique mechanics introduced

- **Repair (L1)** — hold Interact on a broken mechanism; a light diagnostic
  mini-interaction (align/seat parts, supply a part) completes it. No supernatural
  framing yet.
- **Sense Damage (L2)** — a short-range "resonance pulse" that highlights hidden
  breaks, currents, and interactables through surfaces. Traversal + secret-finding
  core tool for the whole game.
- **Healing cost — seeded, not yet spent.** The Animal heal in beat F is scripted
  and shows the *first flicker* of cost (Arlen reels, brief vision-blur). The full
  cost economy (exhaustion/tremors/memory) unlocks with People-healing in a later
  level — here we only plant it.

## 7. Puzzle ideas (concrete)

- **P1 — The Freight Elevator (critical).** Restore a dead lift. Requires routing
  power: a generator runs but its output is mis-connected. Solve by reading the
  panel (or later re-solving faster with **Sense**). Teaches "reconnection," the
  game's core metaphor, on a machine.
- **P2 — Pressure & steam (optional path).** A collapsed side-tunnel is blocked by
  a stuck pressure valve; over-pressurize a boiler *just* to the safe limit to
  blow the debris — overshoot and you scald the path shut for a while. Teaches
  the world's "imperfect, dangerous machinery" tone.
- **P3 — The Sense maze (critical).** In the deep tunnels the correct path is
  invisible; walls hide both dangers and the route. **Sense** reveals a safe
  channel where the Lifeline current still flows cleanly — follow the flow.
- **P4 — Reconnect the growth (optional, lore-rich).** The magical growth has
  strangled a maintenance node. Repair it and you *ease* the growth (it recedes),
  revealing a hidden reward alcove — a gentle first taste of "healing a system,"
  not just a machine.

## 8. Optional paths, secrets & collectibles

- **Optional path A — The Rooftops.** Traverse the gantries above the district for
  a shortcut and a vantage that reveals the map; rewards a **crafting material**
  cache.
- **Optional path B — The Flooded Sub-level.** Below the critical route; gated by
  P2. Contains the level's best secret (see below).
- **Secret — "The Old Ward."** A sealed room behind P4/P2 with a faded **Kufstein
  family marking** — the player can't read its meaning yet, but it's the first
  quiet seeding of the surname reveal. Pure environmental foreshadowing.
- **Collectibles seeded (systems defined later):**
  - **Lore fragments** — 4–6 scattered notes/objects (miner's logs, a doctor's
    frustrated journal about the sickness, a decommission order for the deep
    machine). Feed the eventual **Lore Database**.
  - **Relic rumor** — one note hints a **Whisper Coin** once passed through Cinder
    Hollow (ties to the Seven Relics; a thread, not the relic itself).
  - **Crafting materials** — scrap, conductive wire, crystal shards.

## 9. Mini-boss — "The Overfed Core"

Not a creature with a health bar in the traditional sense. The source machine has
**too much trapped magic** and is lashing out: venting scalding steam, animating
loose debris, pulsing damaging energy arcs on a rhythm.

- **Not a fight — a stabilization puzzle under pressure.** Arlen must **Sense** to
  read which of several conduits is over-pressurized, then **Repair/reroute** them
  in sequence while dodging the timed hazards. Each correct reconnection dims one
  arc.
- **The twist:** the "safe" solution (fully venting it) would kill the district's
  power and the sick elder's life-support. The intended solution is to **rebalance,
  not shut down** — the level's thesis in miniature (WB §11: balance, not control).
- **Cost seed:** the final reconnection briefly overwhelms Arlen (vision-blur,
  stagger) — foreshadowing the healing cost.

## 10. Reward structure

| Reward | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Sense (L2) unlock** | Beat D (critical) | Permanent core traversal/discovery tool. |
| **First healing memory** | Beat F (critical) | Story/emotional payload; opens Arlen's arc. |
| **Crafting materials** | Optional paths & secrets | Feed the crafting/upgrade loop. |
| **Lore fragments** | Exploration | Worldbuilding + Lore Database seeding. |
| **"The Old Ward" secret** | Deep secret | Long-game foreshadowing (Kufstein reveal). |
| **A tool upgrade** (e.g. improved multi-tool = faster Repair) | Mini-boss clear | Tangible mechanical progression. |

Design rule: **critical path teaches; optional path rewards and deepens.** A
player who only mains the story finishes competent; a player who explores leaves
Cinder Hollow with materials, lore, and the first mystery seed.

## 11. Emotional arc of the level

Comfort (home, friend, banter) → curiosity (the sickness) → unease (the deep
tunnels, the growth) → **awe/terror at herself** (the animal heals under her
hands and she doesn't understand it) → destabilization (Lysandra crashes in and
nothing about Arlen's life will be simple again). The player should leave
**unsettled and hooked**, not triumphant.

## 12. Mood, art & audio direction (brief)

- **Palette:** warm amber lamplight and rust up top; cold teal-and-violet magical
  glow in the deep growth. The two palettes meeting = the world's core tension.
- **Audio:** intimate ambience up top (distant hammering, kettle-steam, muffled
  voices); as you descend, music thins to a low mechanical *heartbeat* pulse — the
  first, unremarked echo of the Heart Engine's rhythm. The animal-heal moment goes
  **near-silent**, then a single sustained tone as the wound closes.

4. Script beats F (animal heal) and H (Lysandra collision) as set-pieces.
5. Layer optional paths, secrets, collectibles.
6. Art/audio pass.

Dependencies not yet built: Player Controller, Interaction system, Ability
components, Dialogue system. This doc is the **target** those systems build
toward.
