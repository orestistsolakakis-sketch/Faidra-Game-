# The Heart Engine — Concept Art Prompt Board

> **Ready-to-generate prompts** for the funded AI concept-art pass. Tuned to
> [`ART_DIRECTION.md`](ART_DIRECTION.md) and
> [`levels/CINDER_HOLLOW_ENVIRONMENT.md`](levels/CINDER_HOLLOW_ENVIRONMENT.md).
> Deliberately **original** — no existing franchise is named in any prompt; the
> influences live only in the design brief, not the generation. Concept art and
> any 3D drafts are **engine-agnostic** (usable in Godot now, Unreal later).

**Shared style suffix** (append to every prompt):
> *stylized realism, painterly-cinematic, lived-in hand-repaired industrial
> fantasy, brass and worn copper and rusted iron and old brick, volumetric fog and
> industrial haze, low-key dramatic lighting, warm amber lamplight against cold
> teal-and-violet magical glow, highly detailed, moody, original concept art —
> not photoreal, no text, no logos.*

## Environments

1. **Main street (establishing)** — a narrow, canyon-like street in the lowest
   district of a vast vertical capital; buildings stacked and patched over older
   buildings, roofs joined by makeshift bridges and catwalks, huge exposed steam
   pipes and chimneys, warm lit windows, wet cobbles reflecting lamplight, tiny
   figures of workers; the towering clean city looming far above in cold light.

2. **Arlen's mechanic workshop (interior)** — a cramped workshop built inside an
   old boiler room, a glowing forge, hanging tools, half-repaired machines and
   scavenged parts, warm orange light, steam, a workbench worn smooth by years.

3. **The Scrapyard / Machine Graveyard** — mountains of dead machinery, rusted
   gears and broken automatons half-buried, faint teal magical residue glowing in
   the seams, cold fog, a lonely lamp; beauty in decay.

4. **Rooftops & catwalks at dusk** — a vertical maze of iron walkways, ladders,
   pipes and rope bridges between patched rooftops, laundry strung across gaps,
   blue moonlight and drifting smoke, the warm street glowing far below.

5. **The dead Freight Lift (descent)** — a massive old industrial lift shaft
   plunging into darkness, chains and gears stilled, an unstable magenta glow
   pulsing from deep below, cold and ominous, water dripping.

6. **The Secret Healer Shrine (hidden)** — a small forgotten shrine hidden behind
   the machinery, living Lifeline light in soft teal running through carved stone
   and root-like growth, reverent and quiet, a single warm votive candle.

## Characters

7. **Arlen Kufstein** — a tall (6'1") mechanic and relic hunter, practical layered
   workwear, tool-belt, rolled sleeves, goggles pushed up, capable and guarded;
   warm copper/amber colour identity; grounded-heroic slightly stylized
   proportions; strong readable silhouette; character concept sheet, neutral pose.

8. **Lysandra Kufstein** — a poised royal turned traveller, refined lines dusty and
   travel-worn, upright and deliberate; cool blue-and-violet colour identity; a
   deliberate visual contrast to Arlen; character concept sheet, neutral pose.

## Generation notes

- Prefer a wide/cinematic aspect ratio for environments (e.g. 16:9), portrait for
  character sheets.
- Generate 2–4 variations per prompt, pick the strongest, then iterate.
- Once a look is locked, the same descriptions guide 3D asset drafts and, later,
  real modelling.
