# The Heart Engine — Character Design Bible

> **Canonical character document.** Source of truth for the cast: who they are,
> what they can do, how they grow (both in gameplay and emotionally), and how
> they relate. Must stay consistent with [`WORLD_BIBLE.md`](WORLD_BIBLE.md).
> Every character has **two progression tracks**: *Gameplay* (what the player can
> do) and *Emotional* (who the character becomes).

---

## Design thesis

The entire game is about **healing**.

- **Arlen** heals bodies, animals, machines, magical damage.
- **Lysandra** heals relationships, communities, political systems, historical
  divisions.

Their romance is the most personal version of that theme: they cannot repair the
world alone — they have to trust each other enough to repair it *together*.

Crucially, abilities are **not** discovered all at once. Arlen gradually realizes
that the thing she has always done — fixing what is broken — is part of her
family's ancient inheritance. Lysandra's "power" is not a glowing magical
ability; it grows through **player choices** — her ability to understand people,
systems, and the consequences of decisions.

## ARLEN NAOMI KUFSTEIN — protagonist

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Mechanic / Relic Hunter |
| **Height** | 6'1" |
| **Home** | Cinder Hollow |
| **Ability** | Life Resonance / Healing |

Arlen does **not** begin the game knowing she has healing powers. She believes
she is simply *good at repairing things* — machines, weapons, tools. Eventually
she realizes she is not only repairing objects; she is **restoring broken
systems**.

### Her ability — the core idea

Arlen can sense **what is wrong** with something — not just physically, but the
exact point where a thing has become **disconnected from what it is supposed to
be**. Her power is not traditional "magic healing." It is **reconnection**.

- **A broken machine** — she touches it and feels *this gear is supposed to
  connect here*. She understands the machine instinctively.
- **An injured person** — she senses *this bone is supposed to be here; this
  blood is supposed to be moving here; this life force is slipping away*.
- **An animal** — she senses fear, pain, injury. Harder, because the animal
  cannot tell her what hurts.
- Eventually: **magical systems**, and finally **parts of the Heart Engine**.

### Gameplay progression (the core verb, growing)

| Level | Ability | What the player does | Player perception |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Repair** | Simple machines only | Fix doors, elevators, weapons, generators, vehicles | *"She's just a talented mechanic."* |
| **2 — Sensing Damage** | Feel damage before seeing it | Touch a machine, see flashes of cracks / broken gears / missing connections / mis-flowing energy → reveals hidden paths, secret machinery, old tech | *"Something's unusual about her."* |
| **3 — Healing Animals** | First living thing | An injured animal; she tries ordinary medicine, then something happens — the wound closes, she panics and pulls away, healing stops. She has no idea what she did. | **Emotional turning point.** |
| **4 — Healing People** | Minor injuries, broken bones, internal & magical damage | Every heal has a **cost** scaled to severity: exhaustion, tremors, temporary blindness, pain, memory loss | Makes her **afraid of her own power**. |
| **5 — Healing Machines** | Realizes it's the same ability | Not "fixing" — sensing the machine's original design and reconnecting it. Explains why she was always gifted: she's been using this unconsciously her whole life. | **The reveal about herself.** |
| **6 — Healing Magic** | Restore unstable-magic damage | Repair what medicine and engineering cannot — but **not everything**. Some things are too damaged; some have changed permanently. | Leads to her arc's lesson. |

**The limit that defines her:** Arlen cannot save everyone. She must learn that
healing someone is **not the same as controlling the outcome**.

### Emotional progression
Distrust → Attachment → Fear of loss → Acceptance → **Choosing to heal without
controlling everything.**

## LYSANDRA KUFSTEIN — co-lead / romance

Arlen is connected to **Life**. Lysandra is connected to **Understanding** — and
it is deliberately **not magical or supernatural**. It develops through her royal
education and her personal journey.

### Her ability — Systemic Perception

Lysandra is extremely good at understanding how **systems** work — not machines,
but **people, institutions, power, politics**. She can eventually look at a
situation and understand: *Who benefits from this? Who is being ignored? What is
actually keeping this system functioning? What happens if one part is removed?*
She begins thinking this is simply her royal education; she learns to wield it
independently.

### Gameplay progression (grown through player choices)

| Level | Stage | What it enables |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Royal Knowledge** | History, politics, geography, languages, etiquette, government, law | *Theoretical* — she knows *about* poverty and the lower city but has never lived them. |
| **2 — Reading People** | Notices who is lying, afraid, hiding something, powerful, being manipulated | **Dialogue mechanic** — choose to: ask directly · pretend not to notice · manipulate the conversation · protect the person. |
| **3 — Leadership** | Leadership = understanding what people *need*, not giving orders | Organize food distribution, negotiate with factions, resolve conflicts, protect civilians, make hard calls. |
| **4 — The Power of Her Name** | Royal identity as a **tool** | Her name can open doors, stop violence, forge alliances, threaten — or put her and Arlen in danger. She must decide **how** to use it; sometimes it helps, sometimes it makes people hate her, sometimes revealing it could get them killed. |

### Emotional progression
Sheltered → Questioning → Guilty → Independent → **Choosing her own identity.**

## Why Arlen & Lysandra work together

- Arlen understands **"What is broken?"** · Lysandra understands **"Why did it
  break?"**
- Arlen repairs the machine · Lysandra understands who designed the system and
  why it was abandoned.
- Arlen heals the person · Lysandra understands what caused the person to be hurt.

They are both trying to heal the world — in completely different ways.

## ❤️ The Romance — slow burn (whole game)

| Phase | State |
|---|---|
| **1 — Distrust** | Arlen sees a spoiled royal; Lysandra sees a rude, suspicious mechanic who hates her for no reason. They argue constantly. |
| **2 — Forced Partnership** | Made to travel together; they learn each other's strengths. Arlen finds Lysandra isn't useless outside the palace; Lysandra finds Arlen isn't just cold and aggressive. |
| **3 — Respect** | They rely on each other — Arlen protects Lysandra physically, Lysandra protects her socially and politically. They begin understanding each other without words. |
| **4 — Vulnerability** | Arlen shares something from her past; Lysandra admits she's terrified everything she believed about her family is false. They see each other as people, not symbols. |
| **5 — Romantic Tension** | Jealousy, almost-confessions, sleeping beside each other while traveling, protecting each other, arguments that turn vulnerable, one of them injured, accidental intimacy, the realization they are each other's safest person. |

The pacing target: the **player** feels *"Oh — they're in love"* **before either
of them admits it.**

### The central romance conflict
Not simply "you're royal and I'm poor." It is: **Arlen's family was erased by
Lysandra's family.** Lysandra did not personally cause it, but she *benefits from
the system that destroyed Arlen's family history.*

- Arlen may ask: *"How am I supposed to love someone whose family erased mine?"*
- Lysandra must understand: *"You are not responsible for what your ancestors
  did — but you are responsible for what you choose to do now."*

That is what gives the romance real emotional weight.

## 👥 Supporting Cast

### 1. Arlen's best friend — the loyal chaos character *(name TBD)*
Grew up with Arlen in Cinder Hollow; knows her from before the royal secrets.
**Personality:** funny, loud, reckless, emotionally perceptive, constantly teases
Arlen about Lysandra. The first to notice — *"You look at her differently"* —
which Arlen denies, obviously. Could be a mechanic, smuggler, courier, or relic
hunter.

### 2. Lysandra's childhood friend *(name TBD)*
From the Crownspire; knew Lysandra before she left the palace — represents her
old life. Genuinely loves Lysandra but believes the monarchy is *necessary*,
creating conflict. Might say: *"You think you understand the city now because
you've spent a few weeks in it?"* **Not evil** — genuinely believes they're
protecting her.

### 3. Arlen's former lover *(name TBD)*
**Not** a jealous villain — someone Arlen genuinely cared for. They broke up
because Arlen was emotionally unavailable / kept putting herself in danger / the
ex wanted a stable life / Arlen couldn't stop chasing relics and mysteries. The
ex understands Arlen better than almost anyone — romantic tension without villainy.
Could tell Lysandra: *"She won't tell you when she's afraid. You'll have to
notice."*

### 4. Lysandra's former romantic connection *(name TBD)*
A past courtship — not necessarily serious love. Someone from the royal world
chosen as her expected future partner (a noble, a political alliance, a childhood
friend). Knows the pre-adventure Lysandra and may still care for her. But
Lysandra realizes she never truly wanted the life she was expected to have.

## 🎭 The Antagonistic Force — THE CONTINUANCE

Not "the evil king" — a **faction**. An ancient group that believes the world
must remain **exactly as it is**, because *change creates chaos*. They **know**
the Heart Engine is failing, but believe the solution is to **preserve the
current system at any cost.**

They want to: control the relics · control the bloodlines · control the Heart
Engine · suppress the truth · prevent the public from learning what happened.

**Moral texture:** some members genuinely believe they are *saving the world*;
others simply want power. This makes them arguable, not cartoonish.

## Open threads (to resolve in later passes)

- **The shared surname "Kufstein."** Arlen (Cinder Hollow) and Lysandra (royal
  Crownspire) share a family name — this needs a deliberate canon answer: are the
  two separated bloodlines literally branches of one erased family? Is it a clue
  the player uncovers, a coincidence others weaponize, or a reveal? Strong plot
  potential; flag for the story-structure pass.
- **Names** for the four supporting cast members and Arlen's best friend.
- **The kingdom / capital's proper name** (referenced but unnamed in the world
  bible).
- **Two playable leads?** Lysandra's dialogue mechanic and Arlen's repair/heal
  mechanic imply the player may control both. This is a major architecture
  decision (single protagonist + companion, alternating control, or party) — flag
  for the Player Controller design.
